# Data sources, licences and methods: research of 6 Oct 2026

Done by Claude (two research passes plus live checks). Every row has a source link. **UNVERIFIED** marks what we could
not confirm. Legend: ✅ commercial use allowed · ⚠️ ask first or terms unclear · ❌ non-commercial or closed.

Bottom line: **adsb.lol is the only free ADS-B source whose licence allows commercial use.** Open internet-measurement
data sees Starlink-wide outages but is **blind to localized satcom jamming**. The best open SATCOM signals are orbital
(GEO inspector proximity) and official reporting (ITU Radio Regulations Board).

## 3. GNSS spoofing methods (published)

- **Position jumps:** velocity above ~650 m/s after a Kalman filter, then clustering. [Liu, ION GNSS 2024](https://web.stanford.edu/group/scpnt/gpslab/pubs/papers/Liu_ION_GNSS_2024_ADSB_Spoof_Detection.pdf). Pitfall: data gaps during jamming.
- **Many aircraft at one false point:** SkAI/ZHAW. The [OPSGROUP 2024 guide](https://ops.group/dashboard/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/GPS-Spoofing-Technical-Guide-WG2024-OG2.pdf) counts 41,000 spoofed flights from 15 Jul to 15 Aug 2024. Pitfall: the spoofed-to point is not the transmitter.
- **Geometric vs barometric altitude divergence:** [Lo, ION ITM 2025](https://web.stanford.edu/group/scpnt/gpslab/pubs/papers/Lo_ION_ITM_2025_Russia_Spoofing.pdf) and [Lo, ION ITM 2026](https://web.stanford.edu/group/scpnt/gpslab/pubs/papers/Lo_ION_ITM_2026_Observations_of_Spoofing.pdf). Pitfall: a stale offset can keep the two consistent.
- **NIC/NACp latching:** avionics can hold NIC at 0 until reset after landing, which leaves false flags after the event ends.
- **Receiver-geometry plausibility:** needs four or more receivers, and crowdsourced sensors can themselves be wrong.

### Orbital threats to comsats (GEO inspectors)

- **CelesTrak GP data** ([usage policy](https://celestrak.org/usage-policy.php)): no account needed, checks every 2 h. No explicit licence (UNVERIFIED).
- **Space-Track** ([user agreement](https://www.space-track.org/documentation#/user_agree)): basic TLE/SATCAT may be redistributed with citation under USSPACECOM's blanket approval. Derived analysis needs prior approval. It also warns public TLEs are not for conjunction assessment.

### Official and reported interference

- **ITU RRB Summaries of Decisions:** free PDFs, three meetings a year ([archive](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-R/conferences/RRB/Pages/RRB-Archives.aspx)). Next meeting 26–30 Oct 2026. Reuse licence UNVERIFIED. The ITU's own interference system (SIRRS) is closed.
  - [RRB-102, Jun–Jul 2026](https://itu.int/dms_pub/itu-r/md/26/rrb26.2/c/R26-RRB26.2-C-0017!!PDF-E.pdf): Iran on unauthorized Starlink; Russia alleging Ku-band interference to Yamal 402 at 55E from Ukraine; Lithuania on GNSS jamming near Kaliningrad.
  - [RRB-96, Jun 2024](https://itu.int/dms_pub/itu-r/md/24/rrb24.2/c/R24-RRB24.2-C-0012!!PDF-E.pdf): deliberate interference to Eutelsat/SES broadcast feeder uplinks at 5E/10E/13E/21.5E, geolocated to Moscow, Kaliningrad and Pavlovka.

## 5. ADS-B pitfalls to filter

- **ADS-B version 0:** reports NUCp, not NIC/NACp, so use version 2 only.
- **Rebroadcast positions:** MLAT, TIS-B and ADS-R positions are not the aircraft's own GNSS. Use the `adsb_icao` type only; `~` addresses are non-ICAO.
- **Persistently bad avionics:** the FAA has no public bulk list ([PAPR per aircraft](https://adsbperformance.faa.gov/PAPRRequest.aspx)). Build our own blacklist.
- **readsb's own GNSS-loss flag** (`gpsOkBefore`/`gpsOkLat`/`gpsOkLon`, [source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiedehopf/readsb/dev/json_out.c)): appears in the live API only, not in trace files. Trigger logic: airborne, ground speed above 50, NACp of 2 or less for a sustained count, cleared when NACp returns to 4 or more.
