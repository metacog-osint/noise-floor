Reading the map

  • Hexes are H3 resolution 4, about 1,770 km² each. A hex is coloured only when 5 or more aircraft were in it that day; 3–4 show as low coverage, and 1–2 are left out.
  • Share follows the formula GPSJam publishes: one bad aircraft is subtracted, so a single faulty receiver never colours a hex.
  • Jamming-scale means a reported position uncertainty of 93 m or worse (NACp below 8). "Live" leaves out aircraft still degraded after leaving interference elsewhere; tick the box to include them.
  • Accuracy counts only aircraft that showed 10 m accuracy at least once that day. Storms and SBAS outages show here, rarely on the 93 m measure.
  • Days to try: 2025-10-14 (EGNOS outage; accuracy measure over Europe), 2025-11-12 (G4 storm; accuracy measure over North America), 2026-09-22 (California GPS loss).
