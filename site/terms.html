What the site is

Noisefloor publishes analysis of publicly available data about GNSS interference and maritime activity. It is provided as is, without warranty of accuracy, completeness or availability. Do not use it for navigation or safety-of-life decisions. Positions and other values are what aircraft and ships reported, and those reports can be wrong, jammed or spoofed; the methods and their limits are on the Methods and Validation pages.

Privacy

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Layer choices on the maps are kept in your own browser. Cloudflare hosts the site and handles each request, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Some parts load from other services, which receive your IP address when your browser fetches from them: map code from cdnjs and jsDelivr, Sentinel-2 imagery from Microsoft Planetary Computer, Esri imagery from Esri, and live space-weather data from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center (on the home page). To decide whether Esri imagery may be offered, the site checks the country your connection comes from; it does not store the answer.
